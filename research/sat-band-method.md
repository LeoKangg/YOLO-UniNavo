# SAT score-band university table

Reviewed 22 October 2026. The table reorganizes the existing 20-school research into the user's requested score-band-by-school format. Existing official profiles were checked alongside new searches for missing school composite ranges. Only primary university sources establish numeric ranges.

Nine rows cover every valid SAT score from 1100 through 1600: 1100–1190, 1200–1290, 1300–1340, 1350–1390, 1400–1440, 1450–1490, 1500–1540, 1550–1590 and 1600. SAT totals move in steps of 10. No subjective difficulty labels are assigned.

For each school with a verified composite 25th–75th percentile range:

- **Score advantage:** the entire row is above the school's 75th percentile.
- **Range overlap:** the row intersects the school's middle 50%, including percentile boundaries. Some scores in the row may cross a boundary.
- **Score stretch:** the entire row is below the school's 25th percentile.

These columns describe score position, not Safety/Match/Reach admission decisions. Grade bands, prerequisites, applicant type, school/major, essays, English evidence and entry-year test policy still require assessment. Very selective schools retain a visible reach-planning note even above their 75th percentile. SAT 1600 does not establish admission safety.

Schools may appear in every row, with their position changing as the score band changes. Each school is placed exactly once per row; missing composite distributions appear in a separate unclassified group. An ACT range or summed SAT component percentiles cannot fill a missing composite value.

Each cell initially shows up to four schools, ordered by proximity to the relevant percentile boundary (or midpoint for overlapping ranges). A disclosure shows the remaining schools. This ordering is a display rule, not a ranking. Every school name opens its researched admissions details. The evidence table gives each school's actual composite range, cohort year, admitted/enrolled distinction, campus/college scope and exact source links. Optional-test submitters are not a representative sample of all applicants.

The source image is a layout reference, not evidence. In particular, UCLA's current official policy does not consider SAT/ACT for admission or scholarships, and the UC system's 2026 policy-review FAQ leaves Fall 2028 undecided. UC campuses are outside this project's 20-school comparison and are not assigned invented SAT admission categories. See sat-band-context.json for the exact primary references.

New original-school and selective-school search findings are recorded in sat-band-original.json and sat-band-selective.json. Older official cohorts, when used, keep their actual years; a newer incomplete table does not silently refresh an older distribution.
