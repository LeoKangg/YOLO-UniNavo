# Research coverage and review notes

Reviewed October 21, 2026. Selection prioritized verifiable international admissions information, broad academic options and a spread of institutional visibility/competitiveness. This is not a complete directory or an exhaustive rank ordering.

Nine universities were developed into detailed records: Arizona State, Arizona, Oregon, Colorado Boulder, Michigan State, Indiana Bloomington, Penn State University Park, NYU New York and Iowa State. Kansas and University of Iowa were also screened; their domestic/international criteria and aid deadlines differ, so incomplete screening was not promoted into full recommendations. Iowa State was added for explicit international admission alternatives and transparent curricular requirements. An older Ontario-specific PDF was located but was not treated as a verified current cutoff because the current country-specific Box document could not be read.

Sources are paraphrased summaries, not copied university pages. Each claim references source IDs; source metadata identifies publisher, URL, verification date and cycle. Raw research may include additional structured cohort fields, which remain in the JSON for future maintainers even when the UI renders the concise narrative.

## Second-pass corrections

- College Board’s current body updates the anticipated October 2027 SAT to October 9; a stale search snippet said October 2. The body takes precedence.
- June 5, 2027 SAT release is June 21, not a guessed two-week interval.
- AP school order deadlines are not student deadlines. Local hosts may close earlier.
- Ontario Grade 9 start year matters: the assumed 2024 cohort has different requirements from the 2025 cohort. No later cohort’s financial-literacy rule was imposed on this student.
- IU’s current school admission paths supersede legacy University Division wording for CAS. The two campus-specific subject pages support Sociology and History options.
- Penn State University Park data is not substituted with an all-campus acceptance rate. A major change is not marketed as an admission shortcut.
- NYU campus scope is unresolved for the future testing policy; the UI says to confirm.
- ASU Campus Immersion and Arizona overall acceptance calculations include explicit counts and cohort in west.json. They are not Ontario admit probabilities.

## Reachability

See link-check.json for the exact HTTP results. 134 of 138 distinct source URLs returned 2xx in the recorded run; four returned 403, with no 404/410 observed. The 403 URLs were retrieved through research tools or corroborated through official indexed material. A 2xx response can still redirect or require interactive inspection, so it is never a substitute for claim review.

## Verification scope

Every source reference and required data field is machine-validated. The entire set of consequential claims was reviewed against official pages in the research phase; fields that could not be established are explicitly marked unverified, partial, historical or requiring confirmation. Future policies and final 2028 costs cannot be verified from an October 2026 snapshot.
